Key Insights

  • Channel balance is healthy — App (34%), Online (33%), In-store (33%). Digital already contributes ~67% of net sales.
  • Staples dominate volume but drag margin (19.2%). Stationery (40%), Packaged Food (38%), Bakery (37%) are high-margin but under-penetrated.
  • Top products are essentials: Rice 5kg, Laundry Detergent, Wheat Flour. Strong cross-sell opportunity into higher-margin categories.
  • Customer segments & loyalty tiers contribute almost equally — no single “VIP” concentration yet. ~25% of customers have no loyalty tier.
  • Cancellations highest in Beverages (5.0%) and Fresh Produce (4.7%). Returns highest in Personal Care (3.7%).
  • Geography: Mumbai + Hyderabad + Bengaluru ≈ 44% of sales. Nagpur & Thane show higher spend per customer.
  • Age 26–45 drives ~59% of sales; highest spend/customer in 26–35 (₹786).
  • Discounts: 62% of lines carry a discount (avg 5.4%); 14% of lines exceed 10% discount.
  • Data grain is product-line (1 line per Transaction_ID). True basket-level KPIs require an order/bill ID.

Actionable Recommendations

  1. Margin-mix strategy — Promote Stationery, Packaged Food, Bakery & Home & Utility via recommendations and displays to lift overall margin toward 28%+.
  2. Cross-sell bundles — Pair Staples with high-margin add-ons (Rice + biscuits/chocolate, Detergent + dishwash). Target 5–8% basket uplift.
  3. Cut cancellations in Beverages & Fresh Produce (inventory freshness, delivery SLAs, stock alerts). Aim < 3.5%.
  4. Loyalty activation — Convert the large “None” tier with onboarding incentives into Silver/Gold.
  5. Discount governance — Cap discretionary discounts >10% and monitor category-level rates weekly.
  6. Geographic focus — Deepen assortment in top 3 cities; pilot higher-margin ranges in high-spend cities (Nagpur, Thane).
  7. Data quality ops — Enforce pick-lists & validation at source for Status, Channel, Category, Discount_Rate, dates.
  8. KPI cadence — Weekly: Net Sales, Cancel/Return %, Discount %, Top-10 sell-through. Monthly: Margin by category, Segment/Loyalty, Store ranking.

Limitations & Assumptions

  • Only Completed status included in revenue & profit KPIs.
  • Unit prices & costs taken from Product Lookup; Gross Profit = Net Sales − COGS.
  • Transaction grain is product-line; average line value used as basket proxy.
  • 12 null dates and 80 null Customer_IDs excluded from time/customer joins.
  • Synthetic dataset — demonstrates process, not real DMart performance.
